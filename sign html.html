          About Signtext

          Signtext is a prototype for turning signing into text. It’s designed first for Deaf and hard-of-hearing people to use every day, with large readable captions and nothing hidden behind a smooth sentence.

          Visual first
          Big type, clear contrast, and no sound needed to use any part of it.
          Honest about doubt
          Every line shows how sure it is, and unsure lines are flagged in words.
          Private by default
          In this prototype, video never leaves your browser.
          Not an interpreter
          For medical, legal and other high-stakes moments, a qualified interpreter is the right choice.

          What exists today

          The pages, the live and upload flow, the caption view, saved history and favorites, a small dictionary and a conversation screen.

          What comes next

          Choosing a sign language, connecting a recognition model, testing it with signers, and writing clear privacy rules.

          Privacy policy

          Last updated October 25, 2026. This explains what Signtext does with your information. The short version: in this prototype, your video stays on your device.

          1. What stays on your device

          • Your camera feed and any video you choose to play are handled in your browser. They are not uploaded, recorded or sent to us.
          • Saved items are stored in your browser on this device: your history, favorites, phrases you add, and settings such as caption size, text size, high contrast and default sign language.
          • If you open the site while signed in to claude.ai with Contributor access or higher, those saved items are also kept in your private storage on the hosting platform, so they follow you across your devices. Other visitors and the site owner can’t read them, and video is never part of it.
          • You can delete all of it with “Clear everything” in Settings, or by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

          2. What we don’t collect

          Signtext has no accounts, and its own code doesn’t use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. We don’t set cookies ourselves, and we don’t ask for your name, email or location.

          3. Camera access

          Live mode asks your browser for permission to use your camera. You can say no, and you can withdraw permission at any time in your browser’s settings. The camera turns off when you stop recording or leave the Translate page.

          4. Other services involved

          • Fonts. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads. Google can see your IP address and browser details.
          • YouTube. The Training page lists YouTube videos. If you press play on one, YouTube’s player loads (using its privacy-enhanced address) and YouTube may collect information under its own policies. Source links and “Open on YouTube” cards take you to YouTube.
          • Hosting. The site is hosted by a third-party platform, which may keep standard server logs such as IP address and browser type. We don’t control that.

          5. If recognition is added

          Real sign recognition will probably need to process video on a server. Before that happens, we will update this policy, explain what is collected, how long it is kept and how to delete it, and ask for your permission.

          6. Changes

          We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
